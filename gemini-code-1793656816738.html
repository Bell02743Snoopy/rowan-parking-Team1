Information Guide, Community Survey & Interactive App Prototype

⚠️ Peak Hours Warning: Search delays are highest between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM (peaking from 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM)[cite: 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 16, 17, 18]. Plan to arrive 30–45 minutes early during these windows[cite: 7, 8, 9, 12, 14, 17, 18].

🚗 Commuter Parking Tips

  • Triad Lot & Business Hall: Severe traffic bottlenecks during mid-day transition hours[cite: 12, 16].
  • Rec Center / Lot B & C: Fills rapidly prior to 10:00 AM[cite: 8].
  • Early Morning Strategy: Arriving prior to 8:30 AM significantly improves chances of finding open spaces[cite: 4, 9, 18].

🅿️ Garage & Alternate Options

  • Nexus Garage (Mick Drive): Reliable pay-to-park option near Rowan Boulevard[cite: 4].
  • Townhouse Garage: Dedicated visitor/event parking on Townhouse Drive.
  • Perimeter / Overflow Lots: Use outer lots (Ellis St / West Campus) to avoid central gridlock[cite: 4, 14, 15].

⚠️ Rules & Enforcement

  • Permit Access: Permits grant hunting privileges, not guaranteed spaces in core lots[cite: 4, 8, 11, 15].
  • Avoid Tickets: Parking on yellow lines, blocking lot loops, or taking faculty spots risks $50+ fines[cite: 4, 9, 12, 16].
  • Parking Office: Bole Annex (601 Whitney Ave) or via email at Parking@rowan.edu.
